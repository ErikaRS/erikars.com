### INGREDIENTS
- 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- Lemon juice from half a lemon
- 1 Tbsp tahini

- Arugula
- Chicken breast
- Pasta
- Parmesan
- 
### PREPARATION

Cook chicken breasts (sous vide is good). Cut/shread chicken into pieces. Cook pasta; drain and oil.
Whisk oil, lemon juice, and tahini together to make a dressing; season with salt and pepper.
Mix together chicken, pasta, arugula, and dressing to make salad. Top with shaved parmesan.
